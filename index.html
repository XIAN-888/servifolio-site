Turn your work photos into a clear report for your client.

Create a report, add photos by room or area, and enter your notes. Carry unresolved items into linked visits, keep earlier references separate from this visit’s evidence, and collect feedback through a returnable PDF. Preview before saving or sharing through the iOS share sheet. A finished example is available when you first open the app and in the three-dot options menu.

Updated November 13, 2026

How do I add photos and create a PDF?

Open a report and add photos using the camera, Photos or Files. In each area, add notes, choose a status, and label photos Before, After or Photo. A standard photo report needs a photo. A follow-up can explicitly document missing current evidence in text instead of presenting an older photo as new work. Review the PDF before using Save PDF or Share to choose a destination.

A photo will not import or a PDF will not open.

Open the original photo in Photos or Files and allow any iCloud download to finish. Check the available storage on your iPhone, then import again. If the app identifies a missing photo, replace or remove that photo and generate the PDF again.

How do I enable the camera?

The app asks for camera access when you choose to take a photo. You can manage permission in iPhone Settings under the app's Camera option. You can also choose existing photos through the system photo picker.

Can I update a report after sending it?

Open the report, edit its photos or notes, and generate a new PDF version. Previously saved PDFs keep the contents they had when created. Send the new version when you want the recipient to have the updated information.

How do I start another visit at the same property?

Open the existing report, open its three-dot options menu, then New visit. The new report is linked to its actual earlier visit and reuses the client, address and area names. Unresolved items carry their explanation and independent reference-photo copies; resolved items stop carrying. Review the service date, add this visit’s evidence and explicitly record the result or why an item was not treated. Adding a photo does not resolve an item automatically. Earlier reports and saved PDFs keep their original contents.

How do I organize or recover reports?

Swipe left on a report, then tap Delete to move it to Recently deleted. Open the three-dot options menu on the home screen to find Recently deleted and restore it. Archived reports are available in the same menu. Use Archive to keep older work out of the active list.

Reports in Recently deleted remain stored for recovery. Editable backups include the stored report library, including archived and recently deleted reports.

How do I move editable reports to another iPhone?

Open Settings, choose Save a backup, and save the .photoreport file to Files. On the other iPhone, install Servifolio, open Settings, and choose Restore a backup. You can also open a .photoreport file from Files.

A PDF is a shareable snapshot. A .photoreport backup contains editable reports, photos and reference copies, linked visits, saved PDF versions, frozen feedback requests, returned files and saved feedback. Save a recent backup before changing devices or deleting the app. Keep a pre-upgrade backup too: an older app cannot safely open newly upgraded records, and returning to it requires restoring that earlier backup.

What can I use for free?

Editing and PDF previews are free. Your first three non-sample reports are free to save and share. Each unlocked report can be updated and resent, counting as one report across its exports. Exploring and exporting the built-in sample is free.

Exporting further new reports requires Pro. A cancelled or failed export preserves the free allowance. Creating a New visit creates a new report. A feedback PDF belongs to its report and follows the same export-access rules; importing feedback does not unlock another report. Already-unlocked reports can still be revised and resent, even after annual Pro expires.

How do Pro purchases and restoration work?

Pro offers a yearly auto-renewing subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Both unlock exports for further new reports. The app's purchase screen and Apple's confirmation show the current price and currency.

Use the Apple Account you used to purchase. Open Settings in Servifolio, choose Pro, then Restore purchases. Apple verifies the active subscription or eligible lifetime purchase. Editable reports can be recovered using your own .photoreport backup.

How do I cancel my yearly subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. A yearly subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges renewal within the 24 hours before the end of the period. After cancellation, Pro access continues until the paid period expires.

If you purchase lifetime access while subscribed, cancel the yearly subscription separately in Apple's subscription settings. Deleting the app leaves an active subscription in place until you cancel it through Apple.

How do I change the language?

In Settings, open Language. The default follows your system. You can choose English (United States), English (United Kingdom), Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese. New report labels and newly generated PDFs follow the chosen app language. Your own text and previously saved PDF versions keep their contents.

What counts as this visit’s evidence?

Review the earlier reference and this visit’s evidence separately. The app compares the current photo selected for a follow-up with that item’s stored reference file. If those stored files are byte-for-byte identical, it discloses reference-photo reuse and requires an explanation before a resolved result can use it. Missing current images are disclosed. A different file or its capture date does not prove a new capture, authenticity or completed work; you choose the status and explanation yourself.

How can a client return feedback in a PDF?

Prepare a fillable feedback PDF for the report and choose how to send it. The request keeps the original report, item context and PDF version frozen in your local library. The recipient can fill the text fields and save a copy in a compatible PDF reader, then return it using a method you both choose. Servifolio does not send or collect it through a developer server.

Import the returned PDF locally and preview its known feedback fields against the issued version. Review older-version responses in their original context, save explicitly, and choose which feedback becomes a follow-up. Importing or adding a photo does not resolve work or rewrite an earlier report. This is not a signature and the app does not authenticate who filled the file.

The returned PDF has no editable fields.

A reader may flatten the PDF or fail to save its fields. Keep the returned copy and use manual feedback entry against the original request when needed; review and choose follow-ups yourself. You may also ask the sender to save a copy that retains the form fields. A flattened file is not treated as successfully parsed feedback.

如何添加照片和生成 PDF

打开报告，通过相机、相册或文件添加照片。每个区域都可填写备注、选择状态，并将照片标记为作业前、作业后或照片。普通照片报告需要照片；后续跟进也可用文字明确说明缺少本次证据，不会把旧图冒充新作业。请先查看 PDF，再选择保存 PDF 或分享。

照片导入失败或 PDF 无法打开

先在相册或文件中打开原照片，让 iCloud 下载完成。检查 iPhone 可用空间后重新导入。如果 App 提示某张照片缺失，替换或移除对应照片，再生成 PDF。

如何允许使用相机

选择拍照时，App 会申请相机权限。可在 iPhone 设置中的本 App 相机选项管理权限，也可通过系统照片选择器使用已有照片。

发出的报告还能修改吗

打开报告，修改照片或备注，再生成新版 PDF。之前保存的 PDF 保留生成时的内容，需要让收件人看到更新时，请发送新版。

同一地点下次服务如何继续记录

打开已有报告，在三点选项菜单选择新建到访记录。新报告会与实际来源到访关联，并沿用客户、地址和区域名称。未解决事项会承接原说明及独立的参考照片副本；已解决事项不再承接。核对服务日期、添加本次证据，再由你明确填写结果或未处理原因，添加照片不会自动解决事项。旧报告及已保存 PDF 的原内容会保留。

如何整理和恢复报告

左滑报告，再点删除，即可移入最近删除。在首页三点选项菜单中打开最近删除，即可恢复。已归档的报告也可在同一菜单中查找，较早的工作可以归档收起。

最近删除中的报告会继续保存在设备中，方便恢复。可编辑备份包含保存的报告库，也包括归档和最近删除的报告。

如何将可编辑报告转到另一部 iPhone

进入设置，选择保存备份，将 .photoreport 文件保存到文件 App。在另一部 iPhone 安装 Servifolio 后，进入设置，选择恢复备份。也可以从文件 App 打开 .photoreport 文件。

PDF 保存供分享的报告版本。.photoreport 备份包含可编辑报告、照片和参考副本、连续到访关联、PDF 历史版本、冻结的反馈请求、回传文件及已保存反馈。换设备或删除 App 前，请保存最新备份；升级前也要另存备份。旧版 App 无法安全打开升级后的记录，返回旧版需要恢复升级前备份。

哪些功能可以免费使用

编辑和 PDF 预览免费，前三份正式报告可免费保存、分享。同一份已解锁报告可继续修改和补发，合计只计一份，内置示例可免费体验和导出。

后续新报告的导出需要 Pro。取消或失败的导出会保留免费额度。新建到访记录会建立一份新报告。反馈 PDF 归属其原报告并遵守相同导出权益规则，导入反馈不会解锁其他报告。已解锁报告即使在年费 Pro 到期后，也可继续修改补发。

Pro 如何购买和恢复

Pro 提供自动续订包年和一次性买断，两种方式都解锁后续新报告的导出。当前价格和货币显示在 App 购买页与 Apple 确认页。

请使用原购买 Apple 账户，在 Servifolio 的设置中打开 Pro，再选择恢复购买。Apple 会核验有效订阅或可恢复的买断购买。可编辑报告可通过你保存的 .photoreport 备份恢复。

如何取消包年订阅

打开 iPhone 设置，点击你的姓名，再进入订阅。包年会自动续订，请至少在当前周期结束前 24 小时取消。Apple 会在周期结束前 24 小时内收取续订费用。取消后，Pro 权益持续至已付费周期结束。

已有包年时购买买断，请另行前往 Apple 订阅设置取消包年。删除 App 后，已有订阅会持续有效，直到你通过 Apple 取消。

如何切换语言

在设置中打开语言。默认跟随系统，也可选择美式英语、英式英语、简体中文或繁体中文。新报告标签和新生成的 PDF 使用所选 App 语言，你填写的文字及已保存 PDF 的内容会保留。

什么算本次到访证据

此前参考照片与本次证据分开查看。App 比较该事项所选本次照片与其已保存的参考文件；这两个文件的字节完全相同时，会披露为参考照片复用。若将事项标为已解决，需先填写复用说明。缺少本次图片也会明确披露。不同文件或拍摄日期不能证明新拍摄、真伪或工作完成，状态与说明由你人工选择。

客户如何通过 PDF 回传反馈

为报告准备可填写反馈 PDF，自行选择发送方式。请求会将原报告、事项背景和 PDF 版本冻结保存在本机库中。收件人可在兼容的 PDF 阅读器中填写文本框、保存副本，再按双方选择的方式回传；Servifolio 不会通过开发者服务器发送或收集文件。

在本地导入回传 PDF，对照发出时的版本预览已知反馈字段。旧版本反馈保留其原背景，需明确保存，再人工选择哪些反馈转为后续待办。导入或添加照片不会自动解决事项，也不会改写旧报告。它不是签名，App 也不会认证填表人的身份。

回传 PDF 没有可编辑字段怎么办

阅读器可能将 PDF 扁平化，或未保存填写字段。请保留回传副本，需要时对照原请求手动录入反馈，再自行审核和选择待办；也可请对方保存保留表单字段的副本。扁平化文件不会被当作已成功自动解析的反馈。
