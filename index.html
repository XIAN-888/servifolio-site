Support

Turn your work photos into a clear report for your client.

Create a report, add photos by room or area, and enter your notes. Preview the PDF, then save it to Files or share it using the iOS share sheet. A finished example is available when you first open the app and in the report-options menu.

How do I add photos and create a PDF?

Open a report and add photos using the camera, Photos or Files. In each area, you can add notes, choose a status, and label photos Before, After or Photo. Add at least one photo, then open the PDF preview. Use Save PDF or Share to choose a destination.

A photo will not import or a PDF will not open.

Open the original photo in Photos or Files and allow any iCloud download to finish. Check the available storage on your iPhone, then import again. If the app identifies a missing photo, replace or remove that photo and generate the PDF again.

How do I enable the camera?

The app asks for camera access when you choose to take a photo. You can manage permission in iPhone Settings under the app's Camera option. You can also choose existing photos through the system photo picker.

Can I update a report after sending it?

Open the report, edit its photos or notes, and generate a new PDF version. Previously saved PDFs keep the contents they had when created. Send the new version when you want the recipient to have the updated information.

How do I start another visit at the same property?

Open the existing report, choose its report-options menu, then New visit. The new report reuses the client, address and area names. Add the new visit's photos and notes, then review its service date before exporting.

How do I organize or recover reports?

Swipe left on a report to move it to Recently deleted. Open the report-options menu on the home screen to find Recently deleted and restore it. Archived reports are available in the same menu. Use Archive to keep older work out of the active list.

Reports in Recently deleted remain stored for recovery. Editable backups include the stored report library, including archived and recently deleted reports.

How do I move editable reports to another iPhone?

Open Settings, choose Save a backup, and save the .photoreport file to Files. On the other iPhone, install Servifolio, open Settings, and choose Restore a backup. You can also open a .photoreport file from Files.

A PDF is a shareable snapshot. A .photoreport backup contains the editable reports, photos and saved PDF versions. Save a recent backup before changing devices or removing the app.

What can I use for free?

Editing and PDF previews are free. Your first three non-sample reports are free to save and share. Each unlocked report can be updated and resent, counting as one report across its exports. Exploring and exporting the built-in sample is free.

Exporting further new reports requires Pro. A cancelled or failed export preserves the free allowance. Creating a New visit creates a new report.

How do Pro purchases and restoration work?

Pro offers a yearly auto-renewing subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Both unlock exports for further new reports. The app's purchase screen and Apple's confirmation show the current price and currency.

Use the Apple Account you used to purchase, open the Pro purchase screen in Settings, and choose Restore Purchases. Apple verifies the active subscription or eligible lifetime purchase. Editable reports can be recovered using your own .photoreport backup.

How do I cancel my yearly subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. A yearly subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges renewal within the 24 hours before the end of the period. After cancellation, Pro access continues until the paid period expires.

If you purchase lifetime access while subscribed, cancel the yearly subscription separately in Apple's subscription settings. Deleting the app leaves an active subscription in place until you cancel it through Apple.

How do I change the language?

In Settings, open Language. The default follows your system. You can choose English (United States), English (United Kingdom), Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese. New report labels and newly generated PDFs follow the chosen app language. Your own text and previously saved PDF versions keep their contents.

使用帮助

把工作照片整理成清楚的报告，交给客户。

新建报告，按房间或区域添加照片，再填写备注。预览 PDF 后，可保存到文件，或通过 iOS 分享面板发送。首次打开和首页的报告选项菜单中都可体验示例。

如何添加照片和生成 PDF

打开报告，通过相机、相册或文件添加照片。每个区域都可填写备注、选择状态，并将照片标记为之前、之后或照片。加入至少一张照片后打开 PDF 预览，再选择保存 PDF 或分享。

照片导入失败或 PDF 无法打开

先在相册或文件中打开原照片，让 iCloud 下载完成。检查 iPhone 可用空间后重新导入。如果 App 提示某张照片缺失，替换或移除对应照片，再生成 PDF。

如何允许使用相机

选择拍照时，App 会申请相机权限。可在 iPhone 设置中的本 App 相机选项管理权限，也可通过系统照片选择器使用已有照片。

发出的报告还能修改吗

打开报告，修改照片或备注，再生成新版 PDF。之前保存的 PDF 保留生成时的内容，需要让收件人看到更新时，请发送新版。

同一地点下次服务如何继续记录

打开已有报告，在报告选项菜单选择新建到访记录。新报告会沿用客户、地址和区域名称。加入本次照片和备注，导出前核对服务日期。

如何整理和恢复报告

左滑报告可以移入最近删除。在首页报告选项菜单中打开最近删除，即可恢复。已归档的报告也可在同一菜单中查找，较早的工作可以归档收起。

最近删除中的报告会继续保存在设备中，方便恢复。可编辑备份包含保存的报告库，也包括归档和最近删除的报告。

如何将可编辑报告转到另一部 iPhone

进入设置，选择保存备份，将 .photoreport 文件保存到文件 App。在另一部 iPhone 安装 Servifolio 后，进入设置，选择恢复备份。也可以从文件 App 打开 .photoreport 文件。

PDF 保存供分享的报告版本，.photoreport 备份保存可继续编辑的报告、照片和 PDF 历史版本。换设备或移除 App 前，请保存一份最新备份。

哪些功能可以免费使用

编辑和 PDF 预览免费，前三份正式报告可免费保存、分享。同一份已解锁报告可继续修改和补发，合计只计一份，内置示例可免费体验和导出。

后续新报告的导出需要 Pro。取消或失败的导出会保留免费额度。新建到访记录会建立一份新报告。

Pro 如何购买和恢复

Pro 提供自动续订包年和一次性买断，两种方式都解锁后续新报告的导出。当前价格和货币显示在 App 购买页与 Apple 确认页。

请使用原购买 Apple 账户，在设置中打开 Pro 购买页并选择恢复购买。Apple 会核验有效订阅或可恢复的买断购买。可编辑报告可通过你保存的 .photoreport 备份恢复。

如何取消包年订阅

打开 iPhone 设置，点击你的姓名，再进入订阅。包年会自动续订，请至少在当前周期结束前 24 小时取消。Apple 会在周期结束前 24 小时内收取续订费用。取消后，Pro 权益持续至已付费周期结束。

已有包年时购买买断，请另行前往 Apple 订阅设置取消包年。删除 App 后，已有订阅会持续有效，直到你通过 Apple 取消。

如何切换语言

在设置中打开语言。默认跟随系统，也可选择美式英语、英式英语、简体中文或繁体中文。新报告标签和新生成的 PDF 使用所选 App 语言，你填写的文字及已保存 PDF 的内容会保留。
