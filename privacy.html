Privacy policy

Updated November 12, 2026

Servifolio is an iPhone photo-report app provided by Guoxin Xian. This policy explains how the app, these support pages and support correspondence handle information.

Report content and local processing

The app stores the photos you choose, report titles, client names, addresses, service dates, captions, area notes and statuses, business contact details, drafts and saved PDF versions on your device. It also stores preferences and records of report-export access locally.

You choose photographs through the system photo picker or Files, or take a photograph after granting camera permission. Imported files may contain metadata, including a capture date. Image handling, report editing and PDF generation run on your iPhone. The developer does not receive your report content through the app.

Saving, sharing and backups

When you save or share a PDF or editable backup, a copy goes to the app or location you choose in the system interface. A report may contain photographs, client and address information, notes and the business details you entered. An editable .photoreport backup includes the stored library, including archived and recently deleted reports, their photos and saved PDF versions.

Export destinations handle these copies according to their own privacy practices. iOS and file providers may download selected files from iCloud or another service. App data may be included in device or iCloud backups according to your system settings. Apple and the relevant file providers manage those services.

Purchases

Apple processes payments, subscriptions and purchase restoration. The app uses StoreKit to verify purchase information and determine Pro access. Purchase status and report-export access records support the three-report free allowance and access to reports you have already unlocked. Payment card details and Apple Account passwords are handled by Apple.

Collection and tracking

The app has no advertising or developer-operated analytics. It does not send reports, photos, usage history or preferences to a developer server, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Using the app requires no developer account.

Managing local information

Archiving keeps a report available in Archived. Deleting moves a report to Recently deleted, where its content remains stored for recovery. These actions retain the report and its files on your device. Using Delete App in iPhone Settings deletes the app's local report library.

Original photos, exported PDFs, editable backup files, and device or iCloud backups are managed in their respective apps or system settings. You can manage camera access through iPhone Settings and subscriptions through Apple's subscription settings.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information and attachments you choose to include. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. We retain correspondence while needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Support website

These static support pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or submission forms. GitHub Pages provides hosting and processes page requests to deliver the website under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Contact and updates

Send privacy questions to xiangguoxin520@icloud.com. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date.

隐私政策

更新日期为 2026 年 11 月 12 日

Servifolio 是由 Guoxin Xian 提供的 iPhone 照片报告 App。本政策说明 App、使用帮助网页和支持往来如何处理信息。

报告内容与本地处理

App 在设备上保存你选择的照片、报告标题、客户姓名、地址、服务日期、照片说明、区域备注与状态、商家联系方式、草稿和已保存的 PDF 版本，也在本地保存偏好设置和报告导出权益记录。

你可通过系统照片选择器或文件选择照片，也可允许相机权限后拍摄照片。导入文件可能包含拍摄日期等元数据。图片处理、报告编辑和 PDF 生成均在 iPhone 上完成，开发者不会通过 App 接收你的报告内容。

保存、分享与备份

保存或分享 PDF、可编辑备份时，副本会发送到你在系统界面选择的 App 或位置。报告可能包含照片、客户与地址信息、备注及你填写的商家信息。.photoreport 可编辑备份包含已保存的报告库，也包括归档和最近删除的报告，以及其照片和 PDF 历史版本。

导出后的副本适用对应目的地的隐私规则。iOS 或文件提供方可能从 iCloud 或其他服务下载你选择的文件。根据系统设置，App 数据可能包含在设备或 iCloud 备份中。这些服务由 Apple 或对应文件提供方管理。

购买

付款、订阅和恢复购买由 Apple 处理。App 使用 StoreKit 核验购买信息并确定 Pro 权益。购买状态与报告导出权益记录用于管理三份免费报告额度和已解锁报告的访问。银行卡信息与 Apple 账户密码由 Apple 处理。

收集与追踪

App 没有广告或开发者运营的分析服务，不会将报告、照片、使用记录或偏好设置发送到开发者服务器，也不会跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你。使用 App 无需注册开发者账户。

管理本地信息

归档后的报告可在归档中找到。删除会将报告移入最近删除，内容继续保存在设备中以便恢复。这些操作会保留报告及其文件。在 iPhone 设置中选择“删除 App”，会删除 App 的本地报告库。

原始照片、已导出的 PDF、可编辑备份文件，以及设备或 iCloud 备份，由各自的 App 或系统设置管理。相机权限可在 iPhone 设置中管理，订阅可在 Apple 订阅设置中管理。

支持往来

发送支持邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址，以及你选择提供的信息和附件。这些内容用于回复请求和调查问题，在处理支持或履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。你可以联系我们申请删除支持往来邮件。

帮助网站

这些静态帮助页面没有分析脚本、广告或提交表单。页面由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 为提供网站而处理页面请求，其服务适用 GitHub 隐私声明。

联系与更新

隐私相关问题请发送至 xiangguoxin520@icloud.com。本政策变更时，会在此页更新内容和日期。
